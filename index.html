Preset

Start a drag in your coloured zone. You’ll keep control of your paddle until you lift that finger. The dashed aim line predicts the bounce off your paddle.

Outside Classic, pull your finger toward your goal and release to send a short, curved boost into the arena. For Laser and Absorber, the line runs from your goal centre through your paddle. Slide to aim and lift to fire.

Start near your wall and swipe into the arena past your paddle. The dotted paddle shows the reach, which is half your drag. Lift to sweep through any pucks in that path, or move back behind the paddle to cancel.

Tap the centre button to pause. Hold it for three seconds to shake the pucks. Releasing during the charge cancels it.

P1 W/S + E, P2 ↑/↓ + /, P3 F/G + H, P4 J/K + L. Hold the attack key and release. Hold X to shake. P pauses.

Hit a pickup with a live puck. Player bonuses go to its last hitter. In doubles, teammates share the score and bonus tokens.
